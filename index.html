01 Introduction

Brand Purpose & Foundations

Form & Foundry started from a simple frustration: most branding work stops at a logo, and most strategy work never becomes anything visible. We built a studio that does both.

Mission

Clarity in seconds. Craft for years.

To forge brands with enough clarity to be understood in seconds, and enough craft to be remembered for years.

Vision

A brand as sharp as ambition.

A world where every growing business has a brand as sharp as its ambition — not just a logo, but a full system for how it looks, sounds, and grows.

Contrast Standard

The Ground Contrast Rule

Always use Logo Charcoal on bright, white, or off-white grounds and on glossy warm Artboards 1–12. Always use Logo Off White on dark, black, or Charcoal grounds. This ensures maximum visual impact, mathematical legibility, and strict alignment with the Form & Foundry brand guidelines.

08 Brand Voice

The Sharp Creative Partner

Direct without being harsh, opinionated without being arrogant. Talks like a sharp creative partner, not a corporate vendor.

Writing Principle 01

Lead with a bold claim

Open with a decisive assertion, then back it up with rigorous reasoning. Never hedge or bury the headline.

Writing Principle 02

Punchy cadence & rhythm

Favour short, punchy sentences mixed with an occasional longer one for natural breathing rhythm.

Writing Principle 03

Active verbs only

Use strong, muscular verbs — forge, build, sharpen, shape, ship. Eliminate passive voice and corporate mush.

Vocabulary Architecture

Words to Use vs Words to Cut

✓ Forge, build, ship, sharpen, cut, solid, momentum
Muscular, industrial, action-first
✕ Bespoke, holistic, 360-degree, synergy, disruptive, ninja
Meaningless agency fluff — ban entirely
